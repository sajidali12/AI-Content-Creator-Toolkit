# Content Calendar Template

## Weekly planning checklist

- [ ] Review audience questions and feedback.
- [ ] Check current developments if timely content is part of the plan.
- [ ] Select ideas using the scoring rubric.
- [ ] Balance evergreen and timely content.
- [ ] Check facts, permissions, safety, and disclosures.
- [ ] Batch production where practical.
- [ ] Add captions, titles, descriptions, and accessibility elements.
- [ ] Review comparable results after a consistent interval.
- [ ] Record one learning and one next experiment.

## Status values

Idea · Researching · Approved · In production · Scheduled · Published · Reviewing · Archived

## Notes

Do not treat this calendar as a rigid obligation. Replace or delay a time-sensitive topic if the facts change, the opportunity expires, or production becomes unsafe or impractical.
