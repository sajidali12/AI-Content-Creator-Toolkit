# Example Requests and Response Patterns

These examples demonstrate the skill's behavior. They are not fixed scripts or performance promises.

## Example 1: Beginner with no niche

**User:** I want to start making videos but don't know what to post.

**Good response approach:**
- Ask one useful question: "What are three subjects or activities you could happily talk about or show every week?"
- Offer a low-pressure niche exploration exercise.
- Suggest test videos across 2–3 adjacent themes.
- Explain that early publishing is a learning phase, not a permanent niche commitment.

## Example 2: Travel vlogger

**User:** I run a travel channel in northern Pakistan. What should I film next month?

**Good response approach:**
- Clarify target audience and travel dates only if needed.
- Research seasonal conditions and current access/safety information if making timely claims.
- Suggest a mix: practical route guide, cost transparency, local food, human story with consent, lesser-known stop, and a realistic itinerary.
- Avoid unsupported claims about road conditions, permits, prices, or safety.

## Example 3: Technology creator seeking current ideas

**User:** Give me five current AI topics for short videos.

**Good response approach:**
- Browse current sources and provide dates.
- Verify official availability and distinguish beta from general release.
- Rank by audience usefulness and creator fit, not hype alone.
- Include a fresh angle and source for each.
- If browsing is unavailable, disclose it and offer evergreen alternatives.

## Example 4: Food creator

**User:** I post recipe reels. I need new ideas.

**Good response approach:**
- Consider audience, dietary context, local ingredients, cost, season, skill level, and production effort.
- Offer specific recipes with a distinct payoff: quick lunch, budget meal, technique comparison, leftover transformation, regional dish.
- Avoid unsupported health claims.
- Mention allergens and safe food handling when relevant.

## Example 5: Fitness creator

**User:** Suggest viral weight-loss content.

**Good response approach:**
- Reframe toward responsible, evidence-based fitness and sustainable habits.
- Avoid guaranteed weight-loss promises, body shaming, dangerous restriction, or medical advice.
- Suggest form demonstrations, myth checks with credible sources, realistic routines, and questions to discuss with qualified professionals.

## Example 6: Creator asks for a calendar

**User:** Make me a four-week content calendar. I can publish three times a week.

**Good response approach:**
- Create 12 slots, unless the user requests a different count.
- Balance content pillars and formats.
- Include topic, audience payoff, angle, effort, and intended goal.
- Leave flexibility for timely topics and audience questions.

## Example 7: Analytics

**User:** This video got more views than usual but no new followers.

**Good response approach:**
- Ask for comparable posts and relevant metrics if needed.
- Explore whether the video delivered a one-off payoff or communicated the channel's recurring value.
- Suggest a follow-up that makes the creator's broader promise clearer.
- Do not claim to know the platform's algorithmic reason.
